# Author intent — Literal UTF-8 LaTeX source

This record was reconstructed retrospectively on 2026-10-01 after the work
had already been performed.

The author wanted the reconstructed LaTeX source to contain normal literal
UTF-8 French text rather than historical TeX accent notation such as
`Universit\'e`.

This was intended as a source-readability improvement, not as an opportunity
to rewrite, correct, modernize, or otherwise change the scientific or
typographical content.

The conversion should therefore preserve the document and its behavior while
making ordinary human-readable prose natural to read and edit in a modern
UTF-8 environment.
